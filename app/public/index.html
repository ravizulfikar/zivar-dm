System

Browser extension guide

Installing the extension is optional. Downloads from a URL work without it.

  1. Open the Chrome extensions page

    In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar. Turn on Developer mode at the top right.

  2. Choose the ZIVAR extension folder

    Click Load unpacked in Chrome, then choose this folder.

  3. Connect to the app

    Open the ZIVAR Download Companion icon in Chrome. Paste the pairing code, then click Connect.

After a video starts playing, click Download video at the top right of the player. You can reopen this guide from Browser extension in the sidebar.
